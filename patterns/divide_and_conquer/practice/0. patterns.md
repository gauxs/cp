# Divide and Conquer Patterns Cheat Sheet

## Pattern: Split on Invalid Element

**Use when:** A substring/subarray is invalid because of specific characters; split around them and recurse.

**Pattern:** Find split points, solve left and right segments, combine best answer.

**Key insight:** Bad elements partition the string into independent subproblems.

### Key skills

- locating split boundaries
- combining segment answers (max length, count)

### Problems

1. [Longest Nice Substring](https://leetcode.com/problems/longest-nice-substring/)

---

## Pattern: Mid-Split and Combine

**Use when:** Answer combines left half, right half, and cross-mid interactions.

**Pattern:** Recurse on `[l, mid]` and `[mid+1, r]`; merge cross-boundary counts or sums.

**Key insight:** Cross-mid work is the extra cost beyond plain recursion.

### Key skills

- merge-step counting
- prefix structures for range queries across mid

### Problems

1. [Count of Range Sum](https://leetcode.com/problems/count-of-range-sum/)

---

## Pattern: Two-Arrays Divide and Conquer

**Use when:** Two sorted sequences; answer depends on partitioning one array relative to the other.

**Pattern:** Binary partition on one array; recurse with narrowed ranges on both.

**Key insight:** Median of two sorted arrays is partition + recurse, not a simple merge.

### Key skills

- partition invariant on two pointers
- base cases for small arrays

### Problems

1. [Median of Two Sorted Arrays](https://leetcode.com/problems/median-of-two-sorted-arrays/)
