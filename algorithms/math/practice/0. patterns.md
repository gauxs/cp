# Mathematics Patterns Cheat Sheet

## Pattern: Sieve and Prime Factors

**Use when:** Many values must be tested for primality or decomposed into prime factors.

**Pattern:** Precompute primes with a sieve for a bounded range, or divide by candidate factors through $\sqrt{N}$.

**Key insight:** Every composite number has a prime factor no larger than its square root.

### Key skills

- Begin sieve marking at $p^2$
- Divide out repeated factors
- Count powers of a prime in a factorial

### Problems

1. [Count Primes](https://leetcode.com/problems/count-primes/)
2. [Factorial Trailing Zeroes](https://leetcode.com/problems/factorial-trailing-zeroes/)
3. [Distinct Prime Factors of Product of Array](https://leetcode.com/problems/distinct-prime-factors-of-product-of-array/)
4. [Ugly Number](https://leetcode.com/problems/ugly-number/)

## Pattern: Greatest Common Divisor

**Use when:** A repeated unit, period, ratio, or divisibility condition must hold for multiple values.

**Pattern:** Reduce the values with Euclid's algorithm and interpret the resulting greatest common divisor.

**Key insight:** Common repetition lengths and common divisors are governed by the same maximal divisor.

### Key skills

- Apply Euclid iteratively
- Verify structural compatibility before using a GCD

### Problems

1. [Greatest Common Divisor of Strings](https://leetcode.com/problems/greatest-common-divisor-of-strings/)
2. [Find Greatest Common Divisor of Array](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-greatest-common-divisor-of-array/)
