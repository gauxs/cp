# Kadane's Algorithm Patterns Cheat Sheet

## Pattern: Best Subarray Ending Here

**Use when:** Optimize a sum over one contiguous subarray and extending a partial answer has a constant-size state.

**Pattern:** Update the best segment ending at the current index, then use it to update the global answer.

**Key insight:** Any optimal subarray ending here either starts here or extends the optimal subarray ending immediately before it.

### Key skills

- Handle all-negative arrays
- Track start and end boundaries
- Run the recurrence for both maximum and minimum sums

1. [Maximum Subarray](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-subarray/)
2. [Maximum Absolute Sum of Any Subarray](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-absolute-sum-of-any-subarray/)
3. [Maximum Subarray Sum with One Deletion](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-subarray-sum-with-one-deletion/)

## Pattern: Circular Kadane

**Use when:** A contiguous segment may wrap from the array's end to its beginning.

**Pattern:** Compare the ordinary maximum subarray with the total sum minus the minimum subarray.

**Key insight:** A wrapping maximum is the complement of one non-wrapping minimum segment.

### Key skills

- Detect the all-negative case
- Compute maximum, minimum, and total in one pass

### Problems

1. [Maximum Sum Circular Subarray](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-sum-circular-subarray/)
