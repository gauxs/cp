# Minimum Spanning Tree Patterns Cheat Sheet

## 1. Basic MST Construction
**Find minimum cost to connect all vertices.**  
**Pattern:** Use Kruskal's or Prim's algorithm  
**Key Insight:** Greedy approach works for MST

**Problems:**  
1. [Min Cost to Connect All Points](https://leetcode.com/problems/min-cost-to-connect-all-points/)  
2. [Connecting Cities With Minimum Cost](https://leetcode.com/problems/connecting-cities-with-minimum-cost/)  

**Key Skills:** Kruskal's algorithm, Prim's algorithm, Union-Find  

---

## 2. Critical and Pseudo-Critical Edges
**Identify edges that are essential or optional in MST.**  
**Pattern:** Test edge removal and forced inclusion  
**Key Insight:** Critical edges increase MST weight when removed

**Problems:**  
1. [Find Critical and Pseudo-Critical Edges in Minimum Spanning Tree](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-critical-and-pseudo-critical-edges-in-minimum-spanning-tree/)  

**Key Skills:** MST properties, edge classification, multiple MST construction  

---

## 3. Modified MST with Constraints
**MST with additional constraints or modifications.**  
**Pattern:** Adapt MST algorithms to handle constraints  
**Key Insight:** Apply MST concepts with problem-specific modifications

**Problems:**  
1. [Optimize Water Distribution in a Village](https://leetcode.com/problems/optimize-water-distribution-in-a-village/)  
2. [Min Cost to Connect All Points](https://leetcode.com/problems/min-cost-to-connect-all-points/)  

**Key Skills:** problem transformation, virtual nodes, constraint handling  

---

## 4. MST Variants
**Problems that use MST concepts indirectly.**  
**Pattern:** Recognize MST structure in disguised problems  
**Key Insight:** Many optimization problems reduce to MST

**Problems:**  
1. [Minimize Hamming Distance After Swap Operations](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimize-hamming-distance-after-swap-operations/)  
2. [Number of Operations to Make Network Connected](https://leetcode.com/problems/number-of-operations-to-make-network-connected/)  

**Key Skills:** problem recognition, graph modeling, Union-Find applications  

---

## 5. Hybrid / Advanced MST
**Combines multiple patterns - favorite for deep interviews**

**Examples:**  
1. [Find Critical and Pseudo-Critical Edges in Minimum Spanning Tree](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-critical-and-pseudo-critical-edges-in-minimum-spanning-tree/) - MST Construction + Edge Testing + Multiple MST Comparison  
2. [Optimize Water Distribution in a Village](https://leetcode.com/problems/optimize-water-distribution-in-a-village/) - Virtual Node + MST + Problem Transformation
