# Heap Patterns Cheat Sheet

## Pattern: Top K with Heap

**Use when:** Need the k largest/smallest elements, or maintain a bounded priority set.

**Pattern:** Keep a size-$k$ heap of the opposite polarity (min-heap for k largest; max-heap for k smallest). Push/pop as you scan.

**Key insight:** Only $k$ elements stay in the heap → $O(N \log k)$ instead of full sort.

### Key skills

- choose min-heap vs max-heap for the goal
- size-$k$ eviction (`if (pq.size() > k) pq.pop()`)

## Pattern: Two Heaps

**Use when:** Need a running median or balance of lower/upper halves.

**Pattern:** Max-heap for the lower half, min-heap for the upper half; rebalance so sizes differ by at most one.

**Key insight:** Tops of the two heaps give the median in $O(1)$ after $O(\log N)$ inserts.

### Key skills

- rebalance after every insert
- even vs odd count median formula

### Problems

1. [Find Median from Data Stream](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-median-from-data-stream/)
2. [Sliding Window Median](https://leetcode.com/problems/sliding-window-median/)
